How it works

1
Record your lecture with PLAUD Note and import the audio or transcript.
2
Review the text, pick a subject, and save to get your summary.
3
Take a quiz, reshuffle it, and set reminders to keep practicing.

Private by design

No account and no uploads. Your lectures and quiz data are stored only on this device, and the app works without internet after the first load.
